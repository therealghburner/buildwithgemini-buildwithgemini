# Corporate Procurement Policy & Purchasing Guidelines

## 1. Purchasing Approval Thresholds
- **Tier 1 (Up to $5,000)**: Direct Manager approval required. Single quote permitted.
- **Tier 2 ($5,001 - $25,000)**: Department Head approval required. Minimum 2 competitive vendor quotes required.
- **Tier 3 ($25,001 - $100,000)**: Director of Procurement approval required. Minimum 3 competitive vendor quotes required.
- **Tier 4 (Over $100,000)**: Chief Financial Officer (CFO) and Chief Executive Officer (CEO) dual approval required. Formal RFP process mandatory.

## 2. Standard Payment Terms & Net Terms
- **Default Terms**: Net 30 days from invoice date.
- **Early Payment Discounts**: Net 10 2% discount encouraged where applicable.
- **Extended Terms**: Net 60 days required for high-risk vendors or purchases exceeding $50,000.

## 3. Restricted & Unallowable Purchases
- **Personal Expenses**: Strictly prohibited. Any personal purchase on corporate PO will result in immediate termination.
- **Alcohol & Entertainment**: Requires prior written approval from HR and Finance.
- **Software & SaaS**: All software purchases regardless of amount must undergo IT Security Review before PO creation.
- **Gift Cards & Cash Equivalents**: Strictly prohibited without CFO signoff.

## 4. Vendor Selection & Risk Compliance
- **Preferred Vendor List**: Purchasing agents must prioritize pre-vetted vendors from the Preferred Vendor List.
- **High-Risk Vendor Flags**: Vendors with cancellation rates >20% or late delivery scores >15% are subject to mandatory audit before new PO issuance.
- **Audit Logging**: Any PO exceeding $50,000 or issued to non-preferred vendors must be flagged for audit review.

## 5. Order Cancellation & Return Guidelines
- **Standard PO Cancellation**: Purchase orders may be cancelled without penalty within 48 hours of issuance if unfulfilled.
- **Restocking Fees**: Maximum allowable restocking fee for returned goods is 10%.
- **SLA Breach Penalties**: Vendors failing to meet agreed delivery windows by more than 14 business days are subject to a 5% liquid damages penalty deduction.
